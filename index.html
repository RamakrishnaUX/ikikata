Ways of living,
from Japan

Eighteen Japanese ideas about how to live, and four ways of seeing at the end. Each one explained in plain words, with its real pronunciation, an everyday example, and a small 3D world to touch.

生き方, ikikata, is Japanese for “a way of living”. Tap the characters to hear it said.
